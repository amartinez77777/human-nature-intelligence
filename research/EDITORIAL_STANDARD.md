# HNI editorial standard v1

**Respect the source, distinguish its claims from evidence, and keep uncertainty visible.**

1. Only treat a chapter/subsection title as verified after checking that it appears in the actual PDF; record a PDF locator for its occurrence.
2. Mark Greene’s content as attributed interpretation rather than externally verified psychology or history. No fabricated quotations, invented studies or inferred diagnostic labels.
3. Mark a case `secondary_source_account` when only Greene is inspected; do not move events to `documentedFacts` without independent primary or high-quality historical confirmation.
4. Scientific evidence requires independent source reading, including study method, sample/context, limitations, and evidence assessment. Do not infer agreement from similar terminology alone.
5. For each sourced claim, record the entity, source, subsection, physical PDF zero-based page index, one-based viewer page, and printed page only when independently verified.
6. Use original paraphrases and brief titles, not extended copyrighted excerpts. No full-book text or rendered pages in ZIP.
7. Distinguish observed behavior from speculative motive; exercises must not diagnose or encourage categorical judgment about real people.
8. If a source is inaccessible, label `blocked`. If inspection is incomplete, label `partially_reviewed`/`awaiting_review`. `fully_reviewed` requires chapter-wide coverage and citation QA, not merely its opening.
9. Update research ledger, coverage report, and tests with every editorial addition. Preserve stable IDs.
