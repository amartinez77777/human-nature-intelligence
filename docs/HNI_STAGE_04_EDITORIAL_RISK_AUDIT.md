# HNI Stage 04 — Editorial Risk Audit

This audit records risks in *Greene's attributed interpretations* separately from independently verified history or empirical science. Every new attributed claim remains `evidenceStatus: not_assessed`; no research finding has been fabricated.

| Area | Main misuse risk | Safeguard implemented | Open verification |

## Detailed controls

1. **Observed fact vs author interpretation:** Every newly created historical dossier has `documentedFacts: []` and `verificationStatus: secondary_source_account`. This means Greene's narration has been inspected but not independently corroborated; it is not a denial that events occurred.
2. **Scientific evidence vs provenance:** A directly inspected PDF passage establishes what the author wrote, not whether its causal assertion has independent empirical support. All 8 new claims are `not_assessed`; `research-findings.json` remains empty.
3. **Gender and diagnosis:** No diagnostic criteria or individual psychological scoring has been generated. Law 12 retains Greene's chapter title but does not endorse a universal gender binary, stereotyped trait ownership or heterosexual parent assumptions.
4. **Risk of confirmation bias:** Cases and concepts provide competing interpretations and express the limits of reconstructing private motives from behavior.
5. **Copyright:** Only original interpretation, brief descriptive metadata and references were bundled. The copyrighted full book PDF was excluded.
6. **Unfinished auditing:** No independent science/history citations, DOI claims, behavioral experiment effect sizes, extracted long book passages, or browser results were created.
7. **Testing gap:** Responsive CSS and source panels pass static review only. Compiled rendering, accessibility navigation in a real browser and Safari screen dimensions are NOT RUN because npm packages could not be installed.

## Editorial release decision

Stage 04 author-source integration is **PARTIAL, SOURCE-LINKED AND NON-DIAGNOSTIC**. Laws 09–12 are not fully reviewed; later sections remain queued and scientifically unassessed. Suitable for continued development, not for clinical interpretation or release as independently validated psychology.
