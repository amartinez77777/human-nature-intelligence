# Stage 07 Independent Evidence Audit

**Meaning of statuses:** `limited` means evidence is indirect, narrow, only partly accessible or non-generalizable. `mixed` means the surveyed literature supports part of a proposition while qualifying broader assumptions. Both are separate from Greene attribution. These statuses reflect this project's **bounded review**, not consensus grades for the whole field.

## Specific unsupported leaps avoided

- A faithfully inspected Greene quotation does not empirically verify psychological law.
- A correlation across studies cannot diagnose an individual or prove individual-level causation.
- A review of deception cues is not the same question as detection of concealed ordinary emotion.
- A gender meta-synthesis does not justify compulsory male/female personality archetypes.
- One national cohort study cannot isolate all historical, institutional and age effects.
- An existential psychology theory does not predict a uniform response to death awareness.
