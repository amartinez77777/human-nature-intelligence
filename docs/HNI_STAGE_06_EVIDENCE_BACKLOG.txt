## Stage 07 editorial safeguards

A claim may accurately represent Greene while still lacking scientific support. For every question, distinguish directly inspected book passages, independently verified history, measured findings and editorial synthesis. Record study design, context, constraints, contradictory evidence and review status before changing any evidence classification. Historical motive attributions and population stereotypes require particularly careful treatment.
